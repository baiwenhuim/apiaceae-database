**Welcometo the Apiaceae Database**

Apiaceae (also known as Umbelliferae) is a large family of mostly aromatic flowering
plants, commonly known as the celery, carrot, or parsley family, and simply referred to as umbellifers. Named after the type genus Apium, it is the 16th-largest family of flowering plants, encompassing approximately 3,800–3,820 species across 434–446 genera.

The Apiaceae Database is a comprehensive multi-omics platform dedicated to accelerating research on the biosynthesis of secondary metabolites in medicinal
Apiaceae plants. By seamlessly integrating high-quality genome, transcriptome,
and metabolome data from 33 species, the database enables in-depth exploration
and elucidation of complex biosynthetic pathways, while facilitating cross-species comparative studies.
