Description

Theobroma cacao (Malvaceae), an economically important tropical-fruit tree crop that is the source of chocolate. It is a diploid tree fruit species endemic to the South American rainforests. Cocoa was domesticated approximately 3,000 years ago in Central America. The Criollo cocoa variety, having a nearly unique and homozygous genotype, was among the first to be cultivated. Criollo is now one of the two cocoa varieties providing fine flavor chocolate.
